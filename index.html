// 07  —  FAQ

The questions every contractor asks.

How long does a build actually take?

Most projects launch in 2–4 weeks from our discovery call. Bespoke jobs take longer, but we tell you up front, in writing.

Do I actually own the website?

Yes. The code, the content, the domain — all yours. If you ever leave us, you walk out the front door with everything.

What about hosting?

We offer hosting as part of the optional care plan (~$99/mo, all-in), or you're welcome to self-host on Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare, or anywhere else. Your call.

Can you migrate me off LMN, ServiceTitan, Wix, etc.?

Yes. We migrate your content, set up redirects so you don't lose Google rankings, and time the cutover so there's zero downtime.

What if I need changes after launch?

The care plan covers monthly updates. Outside of that, we work hourly or by small fixed scopes — no retainers required.

Do you do marketing too?

We handle Google Business, local SEO, review collection, and Google Ads landing pages. For broader marketing partnerships, ask in your discovery call.

Why "AI-native" — does that mean my site is generic?

The opposite. AI helps us research your market, draft faster, and iterate more — but every site is shaped, designed, and finished by humans. You're hiring a studio, not a content farm.
